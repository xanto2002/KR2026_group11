## The Scenario
A bartender wants to suggest the right drinks to customers with different
tastes and constraints, such as non-drinkers or vegans.
The knowledge base describes drinks, their ingredients and their flavours,
and a reasoner uses it to classify the drinks and find which ones fit each customer.
### Competency Questions
These are the questions the KB must be able to answer.

1. Which drinks can Sara drink?
2. Which drinks fit both Marco and Sara?
3. Which drinks are vegan?
4. Which drinks are strong (more than one spirit)?
5. Which drinks have a citrus taste?
6. Which mocktails have cola as an ingredient?
7. Which drinks are bitter?
8. Which alcoholic drinks taste sweet?
9. Is there a customer for whom no drink fits? (an empty answer is interesting too)
10. Which non-alcoholic drink could replace a gin tonic? (a substitute with the same flavour)
